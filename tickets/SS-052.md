## Required technical behavior

### R1

Create durable enrichment jobs distinct from mandatory technical import; explicitly enable analysis for selected source(s), chosen provider/model, and permitted preprocessing.

### R2

Before external transfer show whether thumbnails, resized images, full pixels, prompts, or metadata are sent. Strip EXIF/location/private fields unless specifically needed and authorized; user consent covers processing purpose and provider.

### R3

Support analyze new imports, selected assets, and explicit backfill with counts, estimated units/cost when prices are known, unknown-cost labeling, and per-batch/request ceilings.

### R4

Reserve usage budget atomically before dispatch; reconcile reported usage afterward. Unknown provider outcome after a disconnect is not an automatic billable retry; expose review/retry and preserve operation identity.

### R5

Deduplicate work by content/model/preprocessor/schema version but attach results per asset without merging annotations or source permissions. Prevent reuse across forbidden sharing boundaries.

### R6

Prioritize interactive preview/audio/import over enrichment; bound workers, pending payloads, retries/backoff, and log retention.

### R7

Fence publication by source generation/content version and current consent/model config. Handle revocation, restart leases, cancel, offline source, quota/rate limits, model removal, and shutdown.

### R8

Do not claim already transmitted data can be recalled; revocation blocks new dispatch and result publication as specified. Late responses cannot resurrect cancelled/stale jobs.

### R9

Expose visible queue states, per-item sanitized errors, counters, retry options, and source-snapshot evidence persistence extensions.

## Failure, boundary, and verification scenarios

- **T1:** Network instrumentation proves zero user-image transfer before relevant authorization and excludes unapproved sources.
- **T2:** Concurrent budget reservations, timeout with uncertain billing, crash/restart, quota exhaustion, revocation race, duplicate jobs, and changed content.
- **T3:** Stress simultaneous preview/audio/import/enrichment and confirm resource limits and UI responsiveness.
- **T4:** Apply the shared contract's relevant authorization, concurrency, cancellation, persistence, recovery, resource, and accessibility checks. Record a reason for any genuinely inapplicable category.
- **T5:** Verify impacted legacy audio behavior and ensure original media hashes are unchanged. Use real media/native/client tests for media/platform claims, not mocks alone.
