## Required technical behavior

### R1

Implement erase/restore brush with size/hardness/opacity, zoom-aware strokes, invert/reset where useful, and optional feather/edge cleanup with explicitly defined parameters.

### R2

Maintain editable alpha/mask data at original oriented resolution or a lossless tiled representation; preview downsampling cannot become the saved mask.

### R3

Add qualified SS-073 matting where supported, with foreground/background/unknown guidance and explicit rerun bounds. Keep manual brush control usable without matting.

### R4

Use undo/redo bounded by memory/disk checkpoints; group strokes predictably and preserve revision ordering across model suggestions, manual edits, save, and reload.

### R5

Show checkerboard, light, dark, and user-chosen comparison backgrounds; inspect hair/fur, fine lines, holes, partial transparency, and color halos at 100%. No checkerboard pixels enter the mask/image.

### R6

Specify mask value range/bit depth, premultiplication conventions, edge feather behavior, and any color decontamination as an explicit derivative operation. Default source RGB fidelity must remain available.

### R7

Bind refinements to source/content and chosen mask revision; invalidate coordinate-dependent edits on replacement rather than silently applying them to new pixels.

### R8

Keep brush/matting processing bounded off the UI thread, cancellable, and safe under low memory; protect draft persistence from partial writes.

### R9

Provide keyboard-accessible numeric tool controls and non-freehand correction alternatives; document unavoidable limits for occlusion/glass/thin watercolor edges.

## Failure, boundary, and verification scenarios

- **T1:** Golden brush, erase/restore, feather, holes, partial-alpha, and undo/redo fixtures at varying zoom/orientation.
- **T2:** Visual edge review on contrasting backgrounds with held-out hair/fur/thin-line/translucent images.
- **T3:** Crash/reload draft, source change, oversized mask, matting cancel/timeout, conflicting revision, and long undo history.
- **T4:** Apply the shared contract's relevant authorization, concurrency, cancellation, persistence, recovery, resource, and accessibility checks. Record a reason for any genuinely inapplicable category.
- **T5:** Verify impacted legacy audio behavior and ensure original media hashes are unchanged. Use real media/native/client tests for media/platform claims, not mocks alone.
