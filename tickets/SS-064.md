## Required technical behavior

### R1

Implement deterministic checks for target dimensions, crop coverage, enlargement factor, aspect mismatch, and actual transparency against a named output intent.

### R2

Optional blur/compression/artifact hints require a qualified method and held-out photos plus generated art; store method/version/threshold/uncertainty and do not label intentional texture as a definite defect.

### R3

Add full-resolution review navigation and user approve/reject/needs-review notes per asset/output intent; human judgment remains separate from generic image quality.

### R4

Show unsupported/unmeasured conditions explicitly; do not certify hands/faces/text or complete absence of defects from a model response.

### R5

Version findings by content/crop/output settings; changed target dimensions invalidate suitability conclusions without deleting creator notes.

### R6

Integrate filterable review state, inspector explanations, and later shortlist/export warnings; avoid blocking intentional low-resolution/texture choices without explicit product rule.

### R7

Bound optional AI resource/cost usage via SS-052 and persist durable findings/corrections through established metadata paths.

## Failure, boundary, and verification scenarios

- **T1:** Known source/target/crop math, upscaled tiny images, opaque PNGs, intentional blur/grain, illustrations, and real artifact fixtures.
- **T2:** Model absence/failure, changed recipe/output target, and conflicting human reviews.
- **T3:** Published precision/false-positive evidence for any AI hint; deterministic numeric checks have exact expected results.
- **T4:** Apply the shared contract's relevant authorization, concurrency, cancellation, persistence, recovery, resource, and accessibility checks. Record a reason for any genuinely inapplicable category.
- **T5:** Verify impacted legacy audio behavior and ensure original media hashes are unchanged. Use real media/native/client tests for media/platform claims, not mocks alone.
