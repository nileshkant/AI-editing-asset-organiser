## Required technical behavior

### R1

Implement suggested/accepted/edited/rejected states with evidence identity, annotation revision, and a durable rejection record; keep user-owned labels authoritative.

### R2

Define behavior after same-model rerun, newer-model rerun, source-content replacement, and restored sidecars. Rejected suggestions cannot silently reappear as accepted; changed content may offer new labeled suggestions for review.

### R3

Provide side-by-side generated/edited captions, per-tag provenance, batch acceptance/rejection with preview and recoverable history, and uncertain-result review filters.

### R4

Target reanalysis by asset/source/failed state/model/category where supported; explain cost/consent and invalidate only affected generated fields.

### R5

Preserve original evidence without duplicating unbounded histories; define retention policy and exported current/evidence states.

### R6

Use expected revisions to resolve concurrent UI/agent edits; refresh on conflict rather than silently replace tags.

### R7

Update aliases and search-visible effective tags predictably; visibly distinguish machine suggestions from user-confirmed data.

Define the effective-tag projection and invalidation contract now; use it in
the inspector/basic catalog views. Full search indexing consumes it in SS-058.

### R8

Extend portable schemas and restore reconciliation for corrections, rejected states, and manual caption overrides.

## Failure, boundary, and verification scenarios

- **T1:** Correct wolf/dog confusion and rerun same/new models; verify rejected label and manual caption behavior.
- **T2:** Race bulk review, single edit, restore, and late AI response; test undo after another edit.
- **T3:** Restart/reimport/sidecar round-trip and changed-image fixtures retain the documented review semantics.
- **T4:** Apply the shared contract's relevant authorization, concurrency, cancellation, persistence, recovery, resource, and accessibility checks. Record a reason for any genuinely inapplicable category.
- **T5:** Verify impacted legacy audio behavior and ensure original media hashes are unchanged. Use real media/native/client tests for media/platform claims, not mocks alone.
