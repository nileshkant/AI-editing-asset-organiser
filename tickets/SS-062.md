## Required technical behavior

### R1

Identify exact duplicates from verified content digests and suggest near-duplicate/variation groups through SS-060 visual retrieval; use separately labeled group types.

### R2

Define reproducible candidate thresholds and bounded clustering; do not let transitive similarity chains merge visually unrelated groups without review.

### R3

Create user-reviewable stacks with representative/preferred version, compare, split, merge, exclude-pair, and dismiss controls.

### R4

Preserve independent asset identities, paths, tags, usage notes, revisions, and exports. Grouping is not destructive file deletion or metadata consolidation.

### R5

Update groups when files change or go offline; explicit user grouping decisions outlive automatic recomputation unless their members are unavailable, then show state.

### R6

Keep intentional crop/derivative families distinct from accidental duplicates; reserve parent-lineage hints for later SS-077.

### R7

Scope group discovery/counts/representatives to accessible sources; cross-source identical content cannot reveal another restricted file.

### R8

Persist groups/overrides in appropriate portable records without injecting source-external references into single-folder sidecars.

## Failure, boundary, and verification scenarios

- **T1:** Exact copies, re-encoded copies, similar poses, opposite expressions, cropped versions, and transitive-chain adversarial fixtures.
- **T2:** Changed content, missing preferred member, relink, concurrent group edits, and restore.
- **T3:** Unauthorized member/thumbnail/count and no-auto-delete/no-annotation-merge assertions.
- **T4:** Apply the shared contract's relevant authorization, concurrency, cancellation, persistence, recovery, resource, and accessibility checks. Record a reason for any genuinely inapplicable category.
- **T5:** Verify impacted legacy audio behavior and ensure original media hashes are unchanged. Use real media/native/client tests for media/platform claims, not mocks alone.
