## Required technical behavior

### R1

Record candidate commit, artifact checksum, lockfiles, model/pack versions, supported OS/CPU matrix, signing/unsigned-preview status, and bundled dependency/model notices.

### R2

Run fresh/offline install, populated audio upgrade, mixed image import, preview, manual annotation, source relink, portable restore, and export without development tools.

### R3

Exercise enabled AI tagging/OCR, corrections, semantic/reference search, strict filters, crop/suitability, groups/collections, brief shortlists, usage history, and packs.

### R4

Complete a real scoped MCP client search → preview → choose → export → use in an independently rendered small video/project; record actual outputs and rejected access attempts.

### R5

Measure representative 10k/100k metadata search, large-pixel decode memory, import/AI queues, cache limits, prolonged browsing with audio playback, and recovery after crashes/storage removal.

### R6

Evaluate held-out AI corpus and creator task results through packaged runtime; record costs/latency/correction burden, no hidden upload, and disabled-model behavior.

### R7

Verify keyboard/screen-reader/high-DPI/layout and native dialogs/drag handoff on every advertised platform; browser mocks do not qualify installed behavior.

### R8

Map every required preceding criterion to evidence. Keep inherited SS-023/024/031/034 release gaps separate and visible; a smaller explicitly tested image platform matrix does not retroactively certify audio release.

### R9

Fix small qualification defects within this ticket; if a larger prerequisite defect requires its own PR, stop the queue and explicitly amend it before proceeding. Do not jump to extraction.
