- **R1 relink/availability for image sources, stable IDs and generations.**
  - Relink is a preview plus a confirmation. The confirmation queues an exact
    listed verification at the next generation. IDs never change.
  - I: *relink_keeps_ids_and_analysis_discloses_changes_and_rejects_ambiguous_targets*.
    A mixed source has 2 images and 1 real WAV. Results by target folder:
    - Identical copy: 3 present, 2 of 2 sample matched, no consent needed. The
      job is `listed`/`verify`/`relink` at generation 1, ready 3 and reused 3.
      Image and sound IDs, hashes and annotations are unchanged, and the root moves.
    - Changed copy: 1 changed, consent required. Ready 3, reused 2. The changed
      image keeps its ID and notes, gets a new hash and is analyzed again.
    - Partial copy: 1 absent. Confirming without consent gives `INVALID_ARGUMENT`;
      a folder swapped in after the preview gives `SOURCE_CHANGED`.
    - Empty folder: `NO_MATCHING_FILES`.
    - Inside another source, or containing it: `SOURCE_OVERLAP`.
    - The originals' BLAKE3 hashes are unchanged.
  - I: *offline_partial_denied_and_replaced_roots_never_mass_mark_missing*
    covers the probe states `offline`, reconnected `online` (reported once),
    `inaccessible` and `changed`.
- **R2 Refresh discovery versus listed checks; separate exact-byte verification.**
  - I: *quick_check_skips_unchanged_bytes_and_exact_verification_catches_what_stats_cannot*.
    - A quick listed check reuses 2 of 2 without reading bytes. The proof:
      after a same-size, same-timestamp rewrite with the stored stat forged to
      match (Unix), the quick check still reports the old hash.
    - **Verify file bytes** detects the change, keeps the ID and notes, records
      the second version in `image_asset_versions`, retains the old immutable
      analysis, and the old version can no longer be read as current.
  - U: *racy_mismatched_or_rewritten_stats_are_never_trusted*. Stats recorded
    in the same instant as the write are untrusted. A rewrite with restored
    mtime still moves the change time (`ctime`; NTFS `ChangeTime` on Windows CI).
    A different hash, a missing file and a directory are untrusted.
  - I: *listed_check_is_scoped_to_members_and_keeps_missing_distinct_from_decode_failure*:
    exact-file sources reject Discover, and checks never admit siblings.
  - I: *listed_check_of_100k_members_discovers_within_one_bounded_quantum*.
- **R3 opt-in watchers, bounded queues, debounce, stability, coalescing,
  overflow → safe refresh, narrow exact-file scope.**
  - U (`image_watch.rs`, injected clock):
    - *rapid_saves_settle_once_after_quiet_and_stable_stats*;
      *new_event_restarts_debounce_and_stability*.
    - *temp_file_rename_reports_only_the_final_name_and_deletions*.
    - *overflow_is_bounded_and_becomes_one_debounced_refresh* (4,096 cap).
    - *ignored_entries_are_dropped_and_requeue_preserves_work*;
      *observe_distinguishes_files_folders_absence_and_links*.
  - I: *watch_batches_stay_in_scope_skip_app_owned_paths_and_escalate_safely*.
    - Importing never enables watching.
    - The exact-file scope rejects siblings.
    - App-owned and temporary names are rejected. Nested folder prefixes collapse.
    - A deleted folder that held members, or more than 256 paths, becomes a
      complete refresh; for exact files it becomes a listed check.
  - N: *image_watch_real_os_events_settle_into_one_scoped_quick_job*. Five
    rapid writes to `b.png.tmp`, a rename to `b.png` and a `.creativeshelf/`
    write produce **one** quick job selecting exactly `["b.png"]`.
  - N: *image_watch_exact_files_ignore_siblings_and_disconnect_pauses_then_refreshes*.
    Sibling writes queue nothing; a member write queues `["only.png"]`.
  - B/R: watching is an unchecked opt-in and explains its scope.
- **R4 offline, missing, denied, decoder failure and partial scans stay distinct;
  prune only after complete discovery.**
  - Missing or inaccessible members now keep `technical_state`, profile, evidence
    and recipes. Previously they were also marked `failed` and staled.
  - I: *listed_check_is_scoped_to_members_and_keeps_missing_distinct_from_decode_failure*:
    a missing member stays `ready` and keeps its profile. Garbage bytes are
    `failed` but `available`. Counts are separated in the status.
  - I: *offline_partial_denied_and_replaced_roots_never_mass_mark_missing*.
    - Offline: no refresh is queued and nothing is marked missing.
    - Unreadable subfolder (Unix, non-root): `DISCOVERY_INCOMPLETE`; a deleted
      sibling stays `available`.
    - Denied root: `inaccessible`. Replaced root: `changed`, refresh refused.
    - Only a complete walk marks the deleted file `missing`.
  - Existing *root_replacement_and_offline_discovery_do_not_mass_mark_ready_images_missing*
    and *missing_reconciliation_is_scoped_and_partial_symlink_walk_preserves_old_assets*
    still pass.
- **R5 content change invalidates previews, technical, AI evidence and recipes;
  keeps annotations and version history.**
  - This is the existing `register_image_when` path; refresh and relink now
    reach it.
  - I: *quick_check…*. Exact verify gives a new hash; `technical_state` returns
    to ready after reanalysis. Annotations are kept, and 2 versions are recorded.
  - SS-044 previews are content-addressed and refuse a non-current version.
  - A decode failure stales evidence and recipes (unchanged behavior). Absence
    no longer does (see R4).
- **R6 ambiguous renames and overlaps are reconciliation; case-only rename
  handled explicitly.**
  - I: *renames_keep_identity_but_copies_and_hard_links_need_explicit_reconciliation*.
    - A rename keeps the ID and notes.
    - A case-only rename `renamed.png` → `RENAMED.png` keeps the ID on
      case-insensitive APFS. The exact-entry check avoids the case-insensitive
      lookup trap.
    - A hard link becomes a new record, and the original keeps its ID.
    - Copy then delete gives a new record plus one candidate (`same_file: false`).
    - Reattach with stale revisions, or onto an annotated duplicate, returns
      `CONFLICT`. A valid reattach keeps the original ID and notes at the new
      path, deletes the duplicate record and clears the candidates.
  - Overlapping relinks: R1.
- **R7 never process app-owned sidecars, caches, staging or export loops;
  explicit registration of a final export.**
  - The walk and the watcher filter skip `.creativeshelf*`, `.soundshelf*`
    (export staging is `.soundshelf-export-<uuid>`) and editor/download temporaries.
  - An explicitly selected exact file is still imported.
  - U: *app_owned_names_cover_sidecars_staging_and_temporaries*.
  - I: *watch_batches_…*: 1 member out of 4 files written. N: the sidecar write is ignored.
- **R8 watcher lifecycle, status and retry actions, generation fencing.**
  - Every watcher start queues one quick check. A probe every 5 s pauses and
    resumes watching. Shutdown joins the loop before the import lane stops.
  - I: *generation_changes_fence_queued_refresh_and_stale_watch_batches*.
    - One job per generation (a watch batch gets `SOURCE_CONFLICT` and is
      requeued).
    - Relink cancels the queued refresh with `SOURCE_CHANGED`.
    - A batch from the old generation is rejected; scopes move to generation 1.
  - N: disconnect gives `paused`; reconnect restarts watching and queues a quick
    listed check; disabling removes the watcher.
  - R/B: Check availability, Refresh, Check files, Verify file bytes and Relink…;
    actions are disabled while a job runs or the root is unreachable.

## Acceptance

- **A1 bounded, identity- and annotation-preserving.**
  - Bounds: 100,000 members, 32-row discovery transactions, 4,096 pending
    paths and queued events, 256-path batches, 8 relink plans × 5 min, and a
    sample of 32 files or 256 MiB.
  - The 100k listed check discovers in one quantum; see results below.
  - Identity: R1, R5, R6.
- **A2 no partial scan or offline drive mass-deletes.** R4 tests.
- **A3 generation races and the unchanged-file fast path have evidence.** R2,
  R8 and the existing lease and generation tests.
- **A4 every R has implementation and evidence.** Above.
- **A5 durable fields** (schema v14):
  - `image_import_jobs.scan_kind/verify/origin`: defaults keep the old exact
    semantics.
  - `asset_file_stats`, `image_source_monitor`: backfilled from existing jobs
    and image records.
  - Migration is transactional with a pre-upgrade backup. The genuine v10, v11
    and v12 upgrade tests pass, and the legacy fixture helper strips v14 tables.
  - Backups include these tables. Restore validation rejects tampered root
    state, file ID or origin even with CHECK constraints bypassed (I:
    *watch_preference_and_root_state_persist_and_tampered_backups_are_rejected*).
    The watch preference survives restart and restore.
  - Portable catalogs and source snapshots stay gated for image sources until
    SS-048. Stats and the watch preference are machine-local; SS-048 must
    exclude them (see handoff).
  - Permission: refresh and relink are limited to existing grants. The relink
    target comes only from the native picker, and exact-file scopes never widen.

## Review findings and fixes

- **Fixed in this ticket:** `failed_item` marked a missing or access-denied
  member `technical_state='failed'` and staled its evidence and recipes. This
  conflated absence with a decode failure (R4).
- **Fixed during implementation:** the watcher's first probe treated `unknown →
  online` as a reconnect. Reconnect now means a known offline, denied or changed
  state becoming online. Separately, every watcher start queues one quick check,
  because changes made while the app was closed were never observed.
- **Efficiency:** background probes classify the root without the
  per-source count queries. The renderer polls statuses only while Imports is
  visible. Discovery reuses cached prepared statements: 100k-member listed
  discovery went from 9.2 s to 3.6 s in debug, which also speeds up imports.
- **Accepted limitations:**
  - A filesystem that hides change time can defeat a quick check; that is why
    **Verify file bytes** exists and imports always hash.
  - A listed check cannot tell an unmounted nested mount point from a deleted
    subfolder; walks never cross volumes.
  - A Refresh walk now skips app-owned and temporary names. A member registered
    under such a name, by an older folder import or by an explicit exact-file
    selection inside an already-imported folder source, is marked missing after
    a complete Refresh of that folder. Its record and annotations are kept.
- **Observed, not in scope:** at 200% zoom the existing page header's "YOUR
  AUDIO WORKSPACE" eyebrow overlaps the Add files button. This is unchanged
  PageHeader layout.

## Remaining limits and handoff

- **Not verified natively:** the installed app, the native webview, screen
  readers, and drive disconnects on Windows or Linux. CI runs the same core and
  native watcher tests there, but not against a removable drive. Inotify watch
  limits on very large trees are reported, not worked around.
- Network drive discovery, implicit watching and cross-source identity guessing
  are out of scope.

**Handoff to SS-048** (do not implement here):
- Portable or mixed catalogs must carry asset IDs, `annotations`, aliases and
  `image_asset_versions`.
- They must **exclude** `asset_file_stats` (machine-local inode and timestamp
  evidence; meaningless elsewhere, so the first check after import will rehash)
  and `image_source_monitor.watch` and `root_state` (re-enable watching
  deliberately on the new machine).
- Jobs' `scan_kind/verify/origin` are local history and are excluded with job
  history.
